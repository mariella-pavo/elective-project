LEARNING THEORIES

Five Learning Theories

→
Cognitive Learning Theory
Information processing and storage in the mind are the main topics of cognitive learning theory. It places a strong emphasis on comprehending mental functions like memory, reasoning, and problem-solving.
→
Behaviorism Learning Theory
This theory responds to stimuli shape learning. Theorists such as B.F. According to Skinner, this theory places more emphasis on outward behaviors than on inward mental states.
→
Constructivism Learning Theory
Constructivism holds that learners develop their own understanding and knowledge of the world through experiences and reflection.
→
Humanism Learning Theory
The development of the whole person, including emotional health and personal development, is given top priority in the humanism learning theory.
→
Connectivism Learning Theory
Connectivism is a more recent addition that has to do with education in the digital era. It focuses on how networks and technology make knowledge sharing and acquisition easier.
